The idea

Money isn't the destination. Time is.

We spend decades saving for a future where work becomes optional. But some of the years we'd most like that freedom are happening much earlier.

The Midlife Hiatus explores what it could look like to deliberately build enough financial freedom to step away from conventional work for a meaningful period, without waiting for traditional retirement.

Because some years of life can't be postponed.
